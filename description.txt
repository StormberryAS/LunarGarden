LunarGarden – Moon‑Phase Gardening Planner

Core Functionality:
- Suggest planting, watering and harvesting tasks based on the current lunar phase.
- Calendar view with glass‑morphism cards for each day.
- Export to iCal or CSV for personal use.

Why it fits:
- Directly uses MoonApp’s phase data.
- Gives a practical, everyday use‑case for moon enthusiasts.
- Keeps the premium dark‑mode aesthetic.

Potential UI Elements:
- Calendar grid with subtle moon‑phase icons.
- Animated seed‑to‑plant growth bar.
- Simple “Add to calendar” button.
